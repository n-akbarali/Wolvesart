09 / FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our timelines, deliverables, and collaborative process.

01. What is the typical timeline for a complete brand & website project?

A standalone visual identity typically requires 3 to 4 weeks, while a full flagship digital experience (Art Direction, UI/UX, Custom Illustrations, and Interactive Design System) takes between 6 to 9 weeks depending on scope and content readiness.

02. Do you collaborate with international clients outside of France?

Yes. Over 70% of our partners are based in London, New York, Zurich, Dubai, and Singapore. All workshops, design reviews, and asynchronous updates are conducted fluently in English and French.

03. Can we hire Wolvesart strictly for UI/UX Design or Custom Illustration?

Absolutely. While many clients commission us for end-to-end digital flagship projects, we regularly partner with internal product teams to deliver specialized UI/UX systems, bespoke illustrations, or print art direction.

04. What deliverables do we receive upon completion?

You receive full ownership of all source files: structured Figma design libraries, responsive component tokens, vector brand assets, print-ready files, and comprehensive brand guidelines.
